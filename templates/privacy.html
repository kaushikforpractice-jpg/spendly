Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Spendly ("we", "us", "our") helps you record and understand your personal expenses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and a password (stored only in hashed form).
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage and device data: basic technical information such as browser type, IP address and pages visited, collected through server logs and cookies.

We do not ask for, and you should not enter, bank account numbers, card numbers, PINs or one-time passwords. Spendly does not connect to your bank accounts.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the service, including summaries and reports of your spending.
  • To create and secure your account and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To send service messages such as password resets and important policy updates.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that host our infrastructure or help us operate Spendly, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, where required by law, court order or to protect our legal rights.
  • A successor entity, if Spendly is involved in a merger or sale, with notice to you.

4. Cookies

We use cookies that are necessary to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. You can block cookies in your browser settings, but parts of Spendly may then stop working.

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted connections and hashed passwords, to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it by law.

7. Your rights

You may access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent to processing at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

Spendly is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you.

10. Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy can be sent to privacy@spendly.example.
